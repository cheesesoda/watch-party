# Popcorn Club privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

Popcorn Club is an app for watching videos together with friends. It has no central service: every party runs on a server that the host sets up and runs themselves, and the app only talks to the server address you type in. The app's developer does not run that server and receives none of your data.

## What the app handles

- **Server address, password and display name.** You enter these to join a server. The app keeps the address, your display name and a login token on your device so you don't have to sign in again. The password and display name are sent to that server to let you in and show your name to others in the party.
- **Chat messages.** Messages you type are sent to the server and shown to everyone in the party.
- **Microphone audio.** If you allow microphone access and turn on voice chat, your voice is streamed live to the other people in the party. It is not recorded or stored by the app. You can use the app without allowing the microphone; you will join voice chat listen-only.
- **Video playback.** Videos stream from the host's server to your device. Nothing is uploaded from your device.

## What the app does not do

The app has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, contains no third-party SDKs that collect data, and does not sell or share data with anyone. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or files.

## The host's server

Each server is run by the person who invited you. What that server keeps (for example, chat history while a party is running) is up to them, so ask your host if you have questions about their server.

## Deleting your data

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything it stored on your device. For data held on a server, contact the host who runs it.

## Contact
